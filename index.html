ApprovalOS

Mumbai real estate NOC & approval tracker. Flags expiring clearances before they block construction.

ApprovalOS — Mumbai RE Tracker

Real estate projects in Mumbai run on a web of NOCs — from the AAI, Fire Authority, Tree Authority, Environment, and more. Miss one expiry and your construction certificate gets blocked. This dashboard tracks all clearances across multiple projects, surfaces critical deadlines, flags blockers, and shows which stakeholder needs to act. Built as a proof-of-concept for how a ₹50Cr+ problem can be solved with the right system.

AI Sales Governance Platform

Built for a corporate sales team managing leads across multiple salespeople. Tracks the full pipeline, detects duplicate leads using fuzzy string matching (catches "Lewis" vs "Lewish"), flags incomplete records, and gives leadership a live GMV view by stage and industry. The system replaced a messy multi-sheet process and improved data quality significantly. Loaded here with sample data — fully interactive.

Dead on Time — Kill Time Before It Kills You

Fast-paced browser shooter built on HTML canvas. You play as a character surrounded by waves of time-themed enemies — the longer you survive, the harder it gets. Mouse to aim and attack, wasd/arrows to move. The death messages alone are worth playing for. Built in a single sitting as an experiment in browser-based game mechanics.
